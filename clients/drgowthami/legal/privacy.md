This policy explains what information this website handles and why.

## What the booking form collects

The form asks for your name, phone number, preferred date and the reason for your visit. It does not ask for medical records.

When you submit the form, our server checks the details and builds a WhatsApp message. Your browser then opens WhatsApp. **This website does not store your details.** The message reaches the clinic only if you send it.

## Information shared with other services

- **WhatsApp** receives the message you choose to send. WhatsApp's privacy policy applies.
- **Google Maps** loads the map on the contact page. Google may collect data when the map loads.
- **Our hosting provider** keeps standard server logs, such as IP address and pages visited, to run and protect the site.

## Cookies

This site does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies.

## Your choices

You can call the clinic instead of using the form. To ask about information the clinic holds about you, call +91 89148 05523.
